## 2. Technical Nature & Architectural Scope
Vidwr is a **mobile web browser and client-side userscript presentation platform** designed to provide users with an accessible, high-performance, and tactile user experience. 

- **No Stream Ripping or Content Decryption**: Vidwr does not download, stream-rip, decrypt, host, or redistribute copyrighted audiovisual streams.
- **Client-Side Document Object Model (DOM) Styling**: User customizations (such as CSS cosmetic styling, font adjustments, and DOM element positioning) operate purely locally within the user's local WebView runtime environment on the client device.
- **Fair Use & User-Agent Autonomy**: Under established legal precedents (including *Sony Corp. of America v. Universal City Studios*, *Eyeo GmbH v. Axel Springer*, and standard web accessibility frameworks), end-users retain the right to control how web content delivered to their local hardware display is rendered and presented.
